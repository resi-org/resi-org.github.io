Code of conduct

RESI’s workshops, seminars, and community events bring people from different fields and backgrounds together for open inquiry and respectful scientific debate. This code applies to all attendees, speakers, organizers, and staff at these events.

Respectful scientific exchange

Discuss research and disagreements on their merits. Questions, criticism, and differing viewpoints are welcome; direct them toward ideas and evidence rather than personal attacks. Give others the opportunity to speak and participate, and respect personal boundaries.

Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, and retaliation are not acceptable. Examples include:

  • Threats, personal abuse, or demeaning comments directed at someone’s identity or background.
  • Unwanted sexual attention, stalking, or physical contact that is unwelcome or inappropriate.
  • Repeatedly interrupting or disrupting sessions, or targeting another participant in ways that prevent them from taking part.
  • Retaliation against anyone who raises a conduct concern.

Respect others’ boundaries. If someone asks you to stop unwanted conduct, stop.

Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness conduct that may violate this code, or have a question about it, contact conduct@resi.org.
