Privacy

Reading and searching

This website has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or embedded data-collection forms. Its search runs in your browser; search text is not sent to a search provider.

Fonts and portraits are served with the website. The site does not set application cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser stores that preference locally.

Hosting

GitHub hosts this website. Loading a page sends GitHub the usual request information, such as your IP address, requested URL, and browser information. Basic hosting and operational logs may be processed to deliver and maintain the site. GitHub’s privacy policy.

External services

Research links, mailing-list forms, social pages, and online giving take you to other services with their own privacy practices. Mailing-list signup is handled through Google Forms and online giving through Every.org. Email links open your email application.

Contact

For questions, write to hello@resi.org.
