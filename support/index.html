Why support matters now

There is a limited window in which the fundamental architecture and norms surrounding advanced AI are still being determined.

As AI systems become deeply embedded in science, medicine, finance, and everyday life, retrofitting safety mechanisms will become harder. It may also become harder to establish effective legal and institutional safeguards once these systems and the economic dependencies around them are entrenched.

RESI is developing the intellectual foundations for trustworthy AI. The best time to do this work is now, while these systems are being built.

What your support enables

Why RESI

Being an independent nonprofit allows us to ask the questions of what is truly required for increasingly powerful AI to remain trustworthy, controllable, and beneficial without our work being influenced by financial targets or business goals.

Our working groups bring together researchers across disciplines and career stages. This allows for knowledge transfer between senior and junior researchers. And it promotes the exchange of ideas, the formation of new concepts, and healthy debate between different subject matter experts. RESI focuses on bringing senior experts into superintelligence safety, as our work is built on their research and the knowledge they’ve gained throughout their careers.
