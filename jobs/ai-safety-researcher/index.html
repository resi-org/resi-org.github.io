Our research environment

The Institute for Responsible Superintelligence (RESI) is a non-profit research institute dedicated to the urgent task of building the scientific foundations necessary to make superintelligence safe by design.

RESI brings together core researchers, academics, and practitioners through interdisciplinary working groups focused on specific research questions. Typically, 8–12 groups are active at a time. Groups meet regularly, with smaller teams developing results to share with the wider group.

Research at RESI is AI-assisted, with AI-generated suggestions subject to independent examination and testing. Explore our research agenda and working groups.

The role

You will conduct original research developing safe-by-design approaches to AI and, more generally developing the scientific foundations of AI that enable understanding how AI may and may not be safely built. Your research may involve techniques from cryptography, verification, learning theory, or game theory, but it could also stem from areas further afield such as law or philosophy.

RESI researchers:

  • Develop and publish original research and present findings at conferences, workshops, and seminars.
  • Develop open-source code and other research artifacts, as appropriate to their work.
  • Lead or participate in working groups.
  • Contribute to RESI’s collaborative research program through shared inquiry, feedback, and collaboration.

Qualifications and level

Candidates should demonstrate expertise in AI safety and alignment research, a record of original contributions appropriate to their career stage, and the ability to collaborate across disciplines.

Positions are available at the postdoctoral level and above, including equivalent research levels in industry or independent research. Appointment level will reflect research accomplishments, independence, and leadership experience. Candidates from academic, industry, and independent research backgrounds are welcome.

How to apply

Submit your application through AcademicJobsOnline (AJO), including:

  • Cover letter
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Research statement: approximately 1–2 pages describing your previous research and your own contributions, the questions you would like to pursue at RESI, and how those questions connect to RESI’s research agenda.
  • Publication list
  • Two reference letters; a third is optional. Referees submit their letters directly on AJO.
