Join us

RESI’s culture is mission-first: open collaboration and early sharing of ideas. To protect junior researchers, we will actively support their credit and careers.

RESI hosts a small number of researchers and visitors and welcomes inquiries from researchers whose expertise could contribute to RESI’s research, whether or not they have previously worked on AI safety.

Scientists interested in participating can contact hello@resi.org.

The AI safety reading group offers weekly discussions and welcomes a limited number of outside guests. See the schedule and participation details, or complete the interest form.

Join the RESI announcements mailing list

Local students interested in AI safety may also want to explore the Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative.
