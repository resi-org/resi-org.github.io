Alfred W. Bressler Professor of Law at Columbia. Studies evidence and criminal law, with a particular focus on how technological and legal choices shape privacy, secrecy, and accountability. Her work includes the authentication of AI-generated evidence and applications of zero-knowledge proofs to legal verification.
