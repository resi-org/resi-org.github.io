Assistant professor of computer science at Tel Aviv University working on algorithms, data structures, combinatorics, graph theory, cryptography, and the foundations of provable AI safety. Previously a member at the Institute for Advanced Study and an associate research scholar at Princeton University, he earned his PhD at Tel Aviv University.
