# Updates

Every entry must describe a real announcement or published press item and link to its source.

Use only two categories: `From the institute` and `Press`. Preserve full source headlines
in `title`. Optional `homepageTitle` and `homepageSummary` fields provide a shorter title
and one factual sentence for the homepage; the full title, summary and body remain on News.

The homepage displays the three newest updates/events as date, source publication,
linked title and one explanatory sentence. Institute items link to their on-site News
entry; press items link to the attributed external article. “View all news” goes to `/news`.
Categories are internal metadata only; do not display “From the institute” or “Press” labels.
Both homepage and News use Boston Globe or LessWrong as the source for current entries.
Events without a source publication show their date; their descriptions identify the activity.

Do not invent entries, outcome claims or placeholder announcements.
