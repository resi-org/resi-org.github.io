# RESI News reference comparison

Observed November 4, 2026. This table accompanies the [findings and recommendations](report.md). Links identify the inspected organizational listing, with homepage links where needed. Sample labels below are abbreviated for identification, not replacement headlines. All observations concern the retrieved listing text; an absent field is not a claim about inaccessible article pages or dynamic states.

**Length key:** approximate words of separate descriptive copy, excluding title/metadata; `0` means none shown; `unavailable` means no usable sample. One “sentence” may be a fragment or a truncated excerpt. Counts describe the specified samples, not an organization-wide average. Unless a row says otherwise, three listings were inspected. Internal articles, paper links and externally hosted organizational blogs are distinguished. No inference about an automatic shared database is made from matching text.
