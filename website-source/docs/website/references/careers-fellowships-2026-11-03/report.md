## Work at RESI

### Resolution is the principal careers reference

Resolution’s [homepage](https://resolution.org/) has a compact participation link, **Work with us**, which opens its [Careers page](https://resolution.org/careers). In the rendered page, simple linked rows show role, department and location, then dated updates explain hiring status. The page distinguishes active, paused and closed research areas and provides an Ashby fallback when embedded roles fail. [Desktop](resolution-careers-desktop.jpg) and [mobile](resolution-careers-mobile.jpg) captures preserve this pattern.

The linked [Research Scientist posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/resolution/65704205-7ccd-4df6-a7d1-3e3338bd80a8) supplies responsibilities, qualifications, terms and a staged assessment process, with references collected late. Its public application asks for a résumé, professional links, project evidence, motivation and research-area preference. It does not request uploaded recommendation letters in the initial form reviewed. This is one role’s process, not evidence about every Resolution search or a recommendation that RESI adopt its timing.

There is a maintenance warning: the institute’s October 28 update includes active Agent Foundations hiring, while that job’s status text still carries an August 21 update restricting current review to Scalable Oversight. For RESI, keep one authoritative status and reconcile it with the application destination. Do not copy Resolution’s duplicated update history or its terms.

### Other useful careers patterns

- [Transluce](https://transluce.org/about) puts a short recruitment invitation inside its About participation section and links directly to a [Gem board](https://jobs.gem.com/transluce). This is the closest alternative to creating an informational Jobs page immediately. Its open-source participation invitation is separate from employment.
- [METR](https://metr.org/careers) groups role rows and separately labels general interest. [Epoch](https://epoch.ai/about/careers) also distinguishes expressions of interest and explains that they are not reviewed on a regular schedule. An interest route needs its own honest expectation, not an Apply label.
- [Simon Institute](https://simoninstitute.ch/jobs) explicitly states that it has no open positions. [AI Impacts](https://aiimpacts.org/jobs/) distinguishes its absence of current hiring, speculative applications and past vacancies. These are useful empty states, although an old role description should not compete visually with an active one.
- [CHAI](https://humancompatible.ai/jobs/) separates its postdoctoral fellowship, collaboration and internship routes, and identifies the internship round as closed. [IAS](https://www.ias.edu/apply) separates academic membership from [staff employment](https://www.ias.edu/campus-resources/working-at-ias/human-resources/job-postings). Do not make one generic application button serve different appointment types.
- [Frontier Model Forum](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/careers/) uses a dedicated page with a résumé/cover-letter email route. [AI Futures](https://www.aifutures.org/about) links to a public job document from About. The linked document separates a policy research role from general interest and points both to an Airtable form. It explains that only advancing candidates receive a response. The Google Drive connector resolved the document after the web reader returned an empty body; the form itself was inaccessible. These examples show that a substantial recruitment system is not a prerequisite for discoverable participation information.

### Counterexamples and classification boundaries

[LawAI](https://law-ai.org/seasonal-fellowships/), [IAPS](https://www.iaps.ai/fellowship/), [GovAI](https://www.governance.ai/opportunities), [CAIS](https://safe.ai/work/field-building), [CHAI](https://humancompatible.ai/jobs/) and [FLI](https://futureoflife.org/our-work/grantmaking-work/fellowships/) describe programs they run. Their fellowship headings cannot justify calling a directory of outside programs simply Fellowships. FLI explicitly pauses fall 2026 applications; the mere presence of application links is insufficient evidence of an open round.

[Apart](https://apartresearch.com/fellowships/partnered-fellowships) distinguishes its own pathway from programs in which another organization defines projects and mentors teams while Apart administers delivery. Its coadministration is made explicit, and no partnered round is accepting applications. This is not an independent resource listing. [Kairos and Constellation’s Generator](https://generatorresidency.org/) is a jointly operated residency, explicitly credited to both; its Winter 2027 round closed November 1. [Lightcone](https://www.lightconeinfrastructure.com/campus-operations-co-lead.html) mentions hosting MATS at its venue in a job description, which does not establish a current fellowship application route.

[IHES](https://www.ihes.fr/en/applications/) and [Simons](https://simons.berkeley.edu/participate/postdoctoral-research-fellowships) name outside funding for their own hosted posts. Outside funding does not make the opportunity independently administered. [THE NEW INSTITUTE](https://thenew.institute/en/) says its fellowship program ended in summer 2025; old calls must remain historical comparisons.

## Ranked fellowship card alternatives

1. **External research fellowships** — “Explore AI safety research fellowships run by CBAI and MATS.” Use two named program links inside the card. Beneath the CBAI link, optionally add: “CBAI’s Fall 2026 mentor roster includes RESI researchers.” This is the recommended default: it states ownership directly and accommodates their different relationships to RESI.
2. **Research fellowships elsewhere** — “CBAI and MATS run programs supporting research in AI safety. See each program’s research areas, eligibility and application updates.” Use the same direct program links. Ownership is clear, but “elsewhere” can wrongly suggest no RESI mentoring involvement, so this is less suitable.
3. **Fellowship opportunities** — “Programs run by CBAI and MATS offer opportunities to develop AI safety research experience.” Add provider attribution in each linked item and the specific CBAI mentor note. Familiar and compact, but the heading alone is ambiguous within Join us; the explanatory sentence is essential.

These labels and sentences are editorial judgments informed by the comparisons, not universal observed conventions. Avoid **RESI fellowships** and a blanket **Partner fellowships** heading: neither fits both links on the evidence. A whole-card link would be misleading because the card has two independent destinations. No separate fellowship page is needed for two short entries.
