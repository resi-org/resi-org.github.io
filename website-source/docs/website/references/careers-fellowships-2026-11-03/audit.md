# Careers and fellowship coverage across 44 reference organizations

Observed **November 3, 2026** for all rows. See [recommendation and interpretation](report.md). This matrix selects exactly the 37 AI safety/adjacent and seven institute-comparator records from the [100-entry registry](../organizations.json). Registry IDs are retained for traceability. Timaeus and Sequent are names carried with Resolution, not additional audit entries. CAISI’s current official destination uses CAISSI.

Both tracks were attempted for every row. The review covered official home/navigation pages, the linked or discovered pages below, and a focused official-domain search for careers/jobs/opportunities and fellowship/MATS/partner/external terms as appropriate. Linked official recruitment services were read where accessible. Search excerpts were used to discover destinations, not to verify current openings or fellowship ownership. No account, private application, submission or contact was used.

## Classification and coverage

- **1 Own:** an organization’s own program or employment appointment, including its jointly administered programs. A separate application-service domain or outside financial sponsor does not make it an external fellowship.
- **2 Hosted or mentored:** an independently administered program with documented hosting, placement or mentoring involving the organization. Historical participation does not establish future availability.
- **3 External resource:** an independently run program listed as a resource, without a verified placement at the organization for that example.
- **4 Unclear:** the specific placement, hosting or mentoring relationship is not established. State what is known rather than treating ownership and placement as the same question.
- **NF:** Not found in reviewed pages. **Limited:** access or content limits prevent a useful negative conclusion. A row can contain several classifications because it describes several programs.

Career coverage: **39 usable presentation/route reviews, two partial (Apollo, Santa Fe), three inaccessible (EleutherAI, CSER, LCFI)**. Fellowship coverage: **40 substantive official-site reviews, one partial (Santa Fe), three inaccessible**. “Usable” means enough evidence about presentation and destination to compare the structure; it does not mean every job or every application field was inspected.

The **four durable outside-fellowship listings** are MIRI, Redwood, MATS and IAS. Apollo adds one historical educational article; CAIF adds one explicit funding-partnership post. Other joint-program, residency and venue examples are identified separately and are not added to those four. Careers pages can be useful despite having no openings. A blank page or failed widget is not counted as an explicit no-openings statement.
