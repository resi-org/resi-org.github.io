## Visitor findings carried forward

Forethought has a separate [Visiting Scholars section](https://www.forethought.org/about); its profiles explain research interests/work. GovAI and [LawAI](https://law-ai.org/team/) identify visiting appointments within team rosters and distinguish affiliates. Some visiting appointments are remote or year-long; a role label alone does not establish a short physical visit.

[CAIS's 2023 fellowship roster](https://safe.ai/work/philosophy-fellowship) is a meaningful hosted academic cohort including professors and junior researchers, with guest speakers separately labeled. Its fellowship label does not make it irrelevant to visiting research communities.

[Simons](https://simons.berkeley.edu/people/visitors) associates visitors with affiliations, programs and roles. [Perimeter](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/research/visitor-programs/current-perimeter-visitors) and [IHES](https://www.ihes.fr/en/scholars/invited-researchers/) publish dates covering both short and longer research stays. Perimeter's current page included some ended visits; do not assume every listed person is presently onsite.

The archived [THE NEW INSTITUTE roster](https://thenew.institute/en/fellows/visitors/index.html) links people to research programs. Its report explains short advisory visits alongside longer participation. [Santa Fe Miller Scholars](https://santafe.edu/research/initiatives/miller-scholarship) are internally nominated accomplished people contributing broader intellectual exchange, with named appointments. This is a useful analogy for open-ended engagement; the review did not establish a policy of selectively publishing only famous casual guests.

Research purposes can be brief, accurately described discussions or participation; they do not require an invented project. Current RESI visitor inclusion and display remain undecided, and the roster stays hidden. These findings do not select names or approve a policy.
