## Assessment and recommendation

Tentative program is normal, established academic-event language. There is no single mandatory industry label; this purposive sample demonstrates legitimate alternatives and does not estimate their population frequency. Current Simons examples support the simpler Schedule/Agenda treatment, not a claim that Tentative program is Simons’s standard. Historical Simons Wiki working-group schedules also use tentative, but those are not equivalent to modern standalone event pages and do not establish the default.

For RESI’s mostly settled roster/program with adjustable session times, recommend the plain heading Program and retain a visible note beneath it: All times Eastern (Cambridge, Massachusetts). Session times are subject to change. This is an editorial recommendation based on the examples, not an organizer instruction or established fact about how readers interpret tentative. The existing tentative label is defensible; replacing it would simplify the heading without removing the provisional nature of the timetable.
